I work where data science meets finance — building models that forecast markets, and turning financial statements and industry data into decisions people can act on.

**Data Science and Finance at Northeastern University**, where I learned to treat a balance sheet and a training set as two views of the same question. ## Value delivered

**Three deep-learning architectures, head to head** — implemented and compared LSTM, CNN, and BiLSTM models in Python at VisionX to forecast market trends, evaluated across multiple performance metrics rather than a single score.

**Raw market data to model-ready features** — collected and preprocessed historical prices from Yahoo Finance and engineered technical-indicator features into the pipeline, then improved forecasting through feature engineering and hyperparameter tuning.

**A forecast people can actually use** — shipped the models as an interactive Tkinter application so users configure prediction settings and see results visualized, not buried in a notebook.

**Investment cases across multiple industries** — at Analysys International, evaluated investment opportunities from financial statements, public reports, and industry databases, and turned the analysis into recommendations for strategic decisions.

**Risk and opportunity, side by side** — synthesized qualitative and quantitative market information into competitive advantages, business risks, and growth opportunities, presented to the team in structured PowerPoint narratives.
